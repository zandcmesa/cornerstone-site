Design System

The visual language of cornerstonechurchma.org — colors, typography, motion, and the building blocks every page is assembled from. Anything new added to the site starts here, so the site stays consistent as it grows.

Foundations

Color

A deep night-sky base keeps photography and video luminous. Cornerstone blue is reserved for wayfinding — labels, links, and accents — so it always reads as a signal, never wallpaper. Two blues, one rule: Cornerstone Blue fills icons, borders, badges and large numerals; Blue Text is every blue word under 18px — eyebrows, dates, links, outline buttons — because it clears 6:1 on every surface where the base blue falls to 4.3:1 on elevated cards.

Blocks

Buttons

Pill-shaped, understated, one primary action per view. Hover shifts toward Cornerstone blue rather than filling — the dark canvas stays calm. Outline and ghost buttons are text only, so they carry no horizontal padding — they sit flush with the copy or the container edge beside them. Every button and link shows the same keyboard focus ring (last example); never remove an outline without replacing it.

Chips, overflow & truncation

Read-only metadata under a sermon card: pill radius, elevated surface, hairline border, 3px 8px padding at 11px. They are list items, not buttons — the filter bar's active-filter chips are the interactive cousin. Because they sit in a .plain-list, the chip rule is scoped to .sermon-topics .topic-tag so the list reset can't zero its padding.

Cards never grow a second chip row. Add data-chip-row to the list and it collapses to one line: whatever doesn't fit folds into a +N chip that shows the rest in a tooltip on hover or focus, and pins it on tap. The row re-fits when the card resizes. The same window.Tooltip shows the full title of any .truncate heading that was cut to one line — hover the title text, or tab to the card.

Foundations

Motion

Everything on this page animated in using the system itself. Motion is slow, soft, and one-shot — it welcomes rather than performs. Users who prefer reduced motion get an instant, motion-free experience, and the home-page video starts paused for them. Any video that autoplays carries a visible pause control.

Scroll reveal

Content fades up 24px over 0.65s the first time it enters the viewport, then leaves the DOM alone.

data-reveal

Staggered groups

Grids and lists cascade their children at 90ms intervals, capped so long lists never lag.

data-reveal-stagger

Hero choreography

Eyebrow, headline, copy, and actions enter in sequence; the arc draws itself; video drifts slowly.

.hero-in · .kenburns

Hairline dividers

Semi-transparent rules draw outward from center over 1.4s as each section boundary appears.

.divider-top / .divider-bottom

Parallax accents

Decorative layers drift at a fraction of scroll speed. Reserved for atmosphere, never content.

data-parallax="0.1"

Hover states

Cards lift 2–3px with a blue hairline; imagery zooms 4.5% over 0.6s on the same soft curve.

--ease-soft · 0.6s

Foundations

Accessibility

Built to WCAG 2.2 AA and checked with automated and keyboard testing on every page. These are the rules every new block inherits, not a feature bolted on at the end.

Landmarks & headings

Every page uses the same skeleton so screen-reader users can jump straight to content. One h1 per page; eyebrow labels sit beside headings, never instead of them; card titles are h3s under their section's h2.

Keyboard & focus

Everything interactive is a real link or button, reachable with Tab and activated with Enter or Space. One focus ring everywhere: 2px Blue Light, 3px offset. Whole-card targets put the button in the title and stretch it over the card, so there is one tab stop per card and its name is the title.

Link focus text link
:focus-visible · .card-btn · .has-card-btn

Dialogs

Video, event, group, Planning Center and contact modals open through one helper. Focus moves into the dialog, Tab stays inside (including around the embedded players), the page behind goes inert, Escape closes, and focus returns to the card or button that opened it. Each dialog is labelled by its own title and has a named close button.

A11yModal.open(backdrop, { onEscape, initialFocus })

Names & alternatives

Decorative icons are hidden from assistive tech; meaningful images carry descriptive alt text. Form fields have labels (visually hidden where the design hides them). Icon-only controls and repeated buttons get a full name ("Register: High Praise Dance"). Links that open a new tab say so. Loading skeletons are silent; filter counts and toasts announce politely.

Built to grow

One system, every page

Colors, type, spacing, and motion all live in a single theme file. New pages, new ministries, new campaigns — they inherit this language automatically.
