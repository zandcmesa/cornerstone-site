Who We Are

Continuing the Kingdom ministry of Jesus

Cornerstone Church exists to continue the ministry Jesus began — proclaiming good news to the poor, healing the brokenhearted, setting the captive free, and restoring sight to those in darkness.

Rooted in Isaiah 61 and Luke 4, our call is not simply to hold services — it's to be the presence of Christ in our community and our region.

We are a church for New England — committed to the full gospel: salvation, healing, the Holy Spirit, and the soon return of Jesus. Our vision is to see the northeast transformed, equipping and sending believers across the region.

Our Vision

"To see our region forever transformed by the Gospel, cultivating fully devoted, fully equipped, fully effective disciples of Jesus."

What We Believe

Core values

Nine principles that shape how we worship, serve, and live together as a church community.

01
Scripture
The Bible is our foundation — the inspired, authoritative Word of God that guides every aspect of our life and ministry.
02
Holy Spirit
We depend fully on the Holy Spirit — for wisdom, empowerment, and the supernatural grace to fulfill God's call.
03
Love God & Neighbor
Everything flows from Jesus's two great commandments: love God with everything you are, and love your neighbor as yourself.
04
Prayer
Prayer is not a program — it's the posture of a people who know they need God. We are a house of prayer.
05
Kingdom Focus
We exist for something bigger than ourselves. God's kingdom coming on earth as in heaven is our north star.
06
Discipleship
We believe in the slow, faithful work of making disciples — not producing consumers of Christianity but devoted followers of Jesus.
07
Evangelism
Every person in our region deserves to hear the good news. We are sent people — into our neighborhoods, workplaces, and the world.
08
Unity
The witness of the Church depends on our unity. We pursue reconciliation, humility, and oneness across every difference.
09
Passionate Worship
Worship is not performance — it's the overflow of a people who have encountered God. We give our whole selves in praise.
