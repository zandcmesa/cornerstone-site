I

Who We Are

Cornerstone Church is a community of believers in East Longmeadow, Massachusetts, united by our commitment to the Gospel. We are a Foursquare Church dedicated to cultivating fully devoted, fully equipped, and fully effective disciples of Jesus Christ.

Through worship, teaching, and fellowship, we seek to be a light in our region and beyond, supporting one another in faith and extending Christ's love to our neighbors.

II

Sunday Worship

Join us each Sunday morning at 10:00am for worship, biblical teaching, and communion with fellow believers. Our services blend traditional reverence with contemporary expression, creating space for authentic encounter with God.

We welcome visitors of all backgrounds and stages of faith. Whether you're exploring Christianity for the first time or seeking a new church home, you'll find a warm welcome here.
