Cornerstone Design Previews

Three alternate homepage concepts for Cornerstone Church

Design A: Kibbe Dusk

Editorial New England

Mid charcoal-blue dusk palette with sparse magazine-style sections. Full-bleed photography meets oversized stacked headlines.

  • Fraunces + Manrope typefaces
  • Copper accent, fog-cream text
  • Parallax reveal motion

Design B: Sanctuary Type

Liturgical Modern

Deep indigo with warm paper panels alternating. Monumental typography with liturgical restraint.

  • Playfair Display + Outfit
  • Gold scripture accents
  • Slow fade-up entrance

Design C: Sunday Pulse

Kinetic Community

Warm off-white, deep forest, and electric berry. Energy of a living congregation with photo mosaics and movement.

  • Space Grotesk + Source Serif
  • Infinite ministry ticker
  • Uneven photo mosaic
