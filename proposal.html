Digital Services Proposal & Service Agreement

Site launch, migration, ongoing maintenance, and email template services for Cornerstone Church.

This document covers everything Oasis Creative Studios will do to launch and maintain Cornerstone Church's new website — what's included, what's not, how requests are handled, and the terms under which we'll work together. Both parties sign below to make it official.

Part One

Site Launch & Migration

Before the new site goes live, we need to understand the current digital setup and plan the transition carefully. This work is donated in full — there's no charge to move from your current provider to the new site.

Discovery & Transition Planning

Donated

The first step is a short discovery process to map your current setup. This happens before any migration work begins.

1

Discovery

We'll review your current hosting provider, domain registrar, email configuration, and any accounts managed by your current agency (Vimeo, YouTube, Planning Center, Mailchimp). No long meetings — a short written questionnaire or a 20-minute call.

2

Transition Plan

Based on discovery, we'll produce a written plan covering what changes, when, and in what order — including any steps Cornerstone needs to take with its current agency.

3

Staging & Testing

The new site will run at a staging URL for testing before going live. We confirm everything works — sermons, Planning Center integration, contact forms — before the domain switches over.

4

Go-Live

Domain DNS is updated to point to the new site. The old site is retired on a coordinated date. Ongoing maintenance begins the same day.

A note on email: Switching website hosting typically does not affect your domain's email service (staff inboxes, contact addresses). We'll confirm this during discovery. If any email configuration needs to change, we'll walk through it together before anything is touched.

Important: If your current agency manages your domain's DNS, Cornerstone will need to reclaim that access before migration can begin. This is common — we'll identify it in discovery and handle it step by step.
Part Two

Ongoing Maintenance

The following services are provided at no cost to Cornerstone Church for the duration of this agreement. This is a genuine gift — no invoice, no retainer, no catch.

Weekly Sermon Publishing

Donated

Every Sunday sermon will be edited, uploaded, and live on the website by Tuesday. The new site automatically generates a title, scripture reference, description, and imagery from each sermon — no generic thumbnails, no "Sunday Sermon (Jun 28)."

  • Deadline Live on site every Tuesday
  • Process Worship trimmed from Sunday livestream, uploaded to Vimeo, published to site
  • Metadata Title, scripture, and description generated from sermon transcript. Imagery is generated from sermon content or replaced with a custom image if provided by the church.

Page Maintenance & Content Updates

Donated

Announcements, Groups, Events, and other pages kept current as the church's needs change. Requests are handled one at a time, in the order received, through a single designated point of contact.

  • Turnaround 2 business days from request submission
  • Requests One active request at a time, submitted by the designated church representative
  • Point of contact One designated church representative at a time. Changes to this designation must be submitted in writing.
What's included: Content updates — text, images, adding or removing items on existing pages (announcements, group listings, event details, staff bios, etc.).

What's not included: New page layouts, new site features, design changes, or new functionality. Requests of that nature are welcome but fall outside this agreement and would be scoped separately.
