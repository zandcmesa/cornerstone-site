## 2. Brand consistency

| Metric | Value | Source | Year | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Estimated revenue lift from consistent branding | 23% (respondents' own estimate) | Lucidpress + Demand Metric, "Impact of Brand Consistency" | 2016 | verified-snippet; self-reported survey |

Academic: van Riel & van den Ban (2001, *European Journal of Marketing*) found a new logo raised positive associations and reduced negative evaluations; van den Bosch & de Jong, "How corporate visual identity supports reputation"; Keller (1993, *Journal of Marketing*) defines brand equity through awareness (recognition/recall) plus associations, and consistency is the mechanism that builds recognition. None is a church study.

**Good looks like:** one logo file set, one blue, one name spelling, used identically on site, Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Church Center, email header, screens and tap tags.
**Why:** every revenue stat above is self-reported; the defensible claim is Keller's: recognition requires repeated identical cues.

**Authentication (binding):** Google and Yahoo bulk-sender rules effective Feb 2024 require SPF + DKIM, a DMARC record (p=none suffices) with From-domain alignment, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe honored within two days, and spam complaints under 0.3% (target under 0.1%). "Bulk" is 5,000+/day but authentication is expected of every sender. Google reported a 75% drop in unauthenticated mail after requiring auth (helpnetsecurity.com, 2023). Microsoft matched for Outlook on May 5, 2025 (powerdmarc, single source).
**Good looks like:** weekly, same day and time, one headline CTA, under about 200 words; SPF/DKIM/DMARC on cornerstonechurchma.com; judge opens against ~35–40% (inflated) and clicks against ~2.5–3.5%.
**Why:** opens are unreliable now; clicks and unsubscribes are the honest KPI. Nucleus and ReachRight guidance: one point, one CTA, consistent subject-line format.

## 6. Church Center app

Facts (Planning Center help and blog, verified-snippet): a custom primary color for buttons and links (requires the Publishing custom-pages plan; preview in light and dark); it is not possible to create a branded app icon, the app is always "Church Center"; the Home page is built from blocks in Publishing (text, image, button, link, event schedule) and can be made the start page; the first four nav items are visible and the rest sit under "More"; the mobile app is enabled under Customize → Mobile app and shared via a link or QR with the church preselected. Adoption: no Church Center-specific benchmark found; 67% of churches use an app (Pushpay 2025). "69% of members prefer apps" is an unverified market-report claim.
**Good looks like:** brand color set, church logo and header image on Home, Home set as start page, top-four nav = Give, Groups, Events, Watch; QR to the app on screens and tap tags.

## 9. Link previews and icons

Facebook spec (developers.facebook.com, verified-snippet): og:image at least 1200×630 (1.91:1), minimum 600×315 for the large preview, 200×200 absolute minimum, under 8MB; set og:image:width and height; images are cached by URL, so change the filename to update and use the Sharing Debugger. WhatsApp previews need under 300KB. Favicon set: favicon.ico 16/32/48, SVG icon, apple-touch-icon 180×180 PNG (opaque, ~20px padding), manifest icons 192 and 512. No credible study quantifying click lift from og:image was found; treat that as unverified.
**Good looks like:** every page has a unique og:title and description and a branded 1200×630 image; the same mark is used as app icon and favicon.

## 10. Accessibility and legal

- WCAG 2.2 is the current W3C Recommendation (Oct 2023), adding nine criteria including 2.5.8 Target Size 24×24 (AA) and 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured. The DOJ Title II rule (Apr 2024) adopts WCAG 2.1 AA for state and local governments with deadlines in Apr 2026 and Apr 2027. Title III exempts religious entities (42 U.S.C. §12187), so churches are not legally bound; WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA is the de facto standard courts and settlements use.
- Alt text: Facebook and Instagram auto-generate alt text and allow a custom override (Instagram about 100 characters).
- Captions: see muted-video stats above; "80% of caption users are not hearing-impaired" (Verizon/Publicis) is unverified against the original.
**Good looks like:** site audited clean (done Oct 2026), captions on every video, alt text on every social image, 4.5:1 text contrast on slides and graphics.

## 11. Visitor conversion UX

- Stanford Web Credibility (Fogg, 2002; n=2,684): "design look" appeared in 46.1% of credibility comments (verified-snippet via advocacy.consumerreports.org).
- Lindgaard et al. 2006, *Behaviour & Information Technology*: visual appeal is judged reliably at 50 ms exposure (verified-snippet).
- Nielsen Norman Group, "How Little Do Users Read" (2008; 45,237 page views): users read about 20% of words, 28% ceiling; half the words only on pages of 111 words or fewer (verified-snippet). F-pattern (2006; 2017 update).
- Church-specific: 47% of first-time guests look for service times, location and service length (sermonview.com, verified-snippet, unsourced); "Plan Your Visit" framed as the deciding factor (churchtrac.com); Text In Church's 80% claim is unverified.
**Good looks like:** service time + address + "Plan Your Visit" above the fold on mobile; at most about 110 words before the first CTA; a kids page stating check-in, ages and safety; a what-to-expect page covering dress, length, parking, and the walk from lot to seat.

## Scorecard rubric (rate each channel 0–3)

1. **Logo and mark**: 0 none/wrong; 1 old or distorted; 2 correct but inconsistent sizing/background; 3 current logo, correct clear space, on a brand background.
2. **Name, colors, type**: 0 different name; 1 name variants or colors off; 2 minor drift; 3 "Cornerstone Church" + brand blue + brand type everywhere.
3. **Core facts (name, address, phone)**: 0 wrong; 1 one missing; 2 present with formatting variance; 3 identical string to Google Business Profile.
4. **Service time and URL**: 0 absent or wrong; 1 buried; 2 present; 3 above the fold or in the bio with the canonical cornerstonechurchma.com link.
5. **Voice and tone**: 0 off-brand or copy-paste; 1 inconsistent; 2 mostly aligned; 3 matches the warm, invitational voice with clean grammar.
6. **CTA and link hygiene**: 0 broken or none; 1 generic or competing; 2 one clear CTA; 3 one CTA, tracked link, rich preview, QR sized to distance.
7. **Freshness**: 0 more than 6 months stale; 1 more than 1 month; 2 within the month; 3 updated on cadence (site weekly, social 3+/week, GBP posts monthly).
8. **Accessibility**: 0 none; 1 partial alt/captions; 2 most assets; 3 all images alt-texted, all video captioned, contrast ≥4.5:1, WCAG 2.2 AA on web.
9. **Compliance**: 0 unauthenticated email or no SMS consent; 1 partial; 2 SPF+DKIM, STOP/HELP present; 3 DMARC enforced, 10DLC registered, consent logged, quiet hours.
10. **Measurement**: 0 none; 1 platform defaults only; 2 GA4/UTMs on some; 3 GA4 conversion events, UTM on every outbound link, monthly review against the benchmarks above.
